About these diagrams

  • Each card shows the typical profile the Bodemkaart assigns to a map unit, not a measurement at a location. Boundaries, depths and properties are ranges or mid-values from the map's standard profiles.
  • The data has no colour field. Colours come from the estimation rules above and should be reviewed by soil scientists.
  • Data: BRO Bodemkaart (SGM) V2025-01, Wageningen Environmental Research / BRO, CC0. Dutch soil names are the official legend names.
